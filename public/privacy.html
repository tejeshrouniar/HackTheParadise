Privacy policy

Hack The Paradise is a coding adventure you can explore as a guest or use with an account to sync your progress.

Google sign-in

When you choose Google sign-in, Supabase Auth uses your Google account ID and email to identify your account. It receives basic profile information such as your name and photo, which can prefill your in-game profile. Hack The Paradise uses this information for account access and the player profile. It does not request access to your Gmail, Drive, or other Google content.

Game and profile data

Supabase stores account data such as your profile, progress, preferences, and Sparks. Your username, display name, photo, bio, and community posts may be visible to signed-in players. You can edit your profile from the Wizard Profile book. The site also uses browser storage for your session, device preferences, and local game data.

If you join the leaderboard, signed-in players can see your username, ranking, and counts of synced levels you cleared. You can hide your ranking at any time from the Leaderboard book.

Account controls and contact

You can sign out of the current browser from Wizard Profile or Account & Sparks. You can revoke Google access through your Google Account connections. For questions or requests about your account data, use the support email listed on the Google consent screen or the site's contact page. Keep personal account information out of public feedback.
